§1The plan

Yarden and Ayelet's split, which is the official FFRandonnée staging. Times are guidebook times plus 20 %. In the park core the tent may only stand from 19:00 to 09:00 and sunset is about 19:45, so arrive by 18:00 and cook first. The GPX layers in your Downloads folder match these nights.

One position fix, no tracking: marks finished days done and fills in today's distance, ascent left and arrival estimate. Long-press to pick your position on the map instead. Tap a day title to see it on the map.

D0FRI 11 SEP

Land in Nice 09:45 → a few hours in town → bus 90 → Saint-Dalmas

bus 90 leaves 16:40, ≈2 hhostel check-in closes 19:00

The tight bit. Bus 90 runs Nice to La Bolline three times a day on weekdays, at 08:10, 16:40 and 18:40 from Les Arboras, passing Grand Arénas a few minutes later. The 16:40 arrives around 18:40; the 18:40 arrives after check-in closes. Take the 16:40, be at Grand Arénas by 16:15, and phone the hostel on +33 4 22 49 01 06 in the morning to say you arrive about 18:45. Booked: twin room at HI hostel "Le Chalet", breakfast included, restaurant on site for dinner, check-out by 10:00.

Nice, 10:30 to 16:00. Tram 2 from the airport to Grand Arénas is one stop, so leave the packs in a locker there or carry them. Buy four days of food and a gas cartridge, which cannot fly with you, at the Decathlon or a supermarket near Grand Arénas. Then tram into the old town: Cours Saleya market for lunch, the Castle Hill viewpoint, the Promenade. Be back on tram 2 by 15:45.

D1SAT 12 SEP

Saint-Dalmas → Col de Veillos → Col du Barn → Salèse valley → Le Boréon

≈18 km+1,350 / −1,150 m9 h official, plan 10–11tent by the lake at Le Boréon, ≈1,500 m

The longest day, straight after travel. Breakfast at 07:00, walk by 08:00, and you arrive around 18:30. Water at a spring on the way up and at Lac Petit; nothing on the col. Col du Barn (2,452 m) is the park boundary; the trail then swings through the Salèse valley on the line restored after Storm Alex. Le Boréon is outside the core, so no 19:00 rule: the lake is beside the road, and one report paid €10 to pitch at the horse ranch. Dinner out 1: the hotel-restaurant at Le Boréon. If you are behind schedule at Lac Petit by 14:00, sleep there and finish the stage in the morning.

D2SUN 13 SEP

Le Boréon → Lac de Trécolpas → Pas des Ladres → Madone de Fenestre

≈9 km+1,000 / −600 m5.5 h official, plan 6–7refuge bed or Lac de Fenestre, see below

Short and steep. Trécolpas to Pas des Ladres (2,448 m) is one of the three sections the French guides flag. Camping is banned around Madone de Fenestre. Two legal options: a bed at the refuge (62 places, open to 30 September, book online, dinner served), or carry on one hour to Lac de Fenestre (2,266 m) and bivouac there inside the core. The refuge bed is the comfortable choice and shortens day 3 by nothing; the lake shortens day 3 by an hour.

D3MON 14 SEP

Madone de Fenestre → Pas du Mont Colomb → Refuge de Nice

≈7 km+700 / −350 m4.5 h official, plan 5–6tent by Lac de la Fous, ≈2,230 m

The technical day. Pas du Mont Colomb (2,548 m): scree to a narrow breach with a 2–3 m rock step, fine dry, not in rain. Boulders down to the refuge; the footbridge is gone, so a ford. Short day, so this is where Mont Colomb (2,816 m, off-trail scramble, 2–3 h) fits if the rock is dry and you have the topo.

D4TUE 15 SEP

Refuge de Nice → Baisse du Basto → Refuge des Merveilles

≈11 km+800 / −900 m5–6 htent only on the marked area behind the refuge

Over the high point (2,693 m) into the Vallée des Merveilles: marked trail only, rubber tips on poles, no touching engravings. Afternoon: Mont Bégo (2,872 m, +750 m, easy, sea view) or the guided engraving walk. Refuge dinner available, cash only.

D5WED 16 SEP

Merveilles → Pas du Diable → Authion ridge → Camp d'Argent

≈18 km+700 / −1,050 m7–8 hgîte or tent, 1,750 m, outside the core

No water after the refuge until Camp d'Argent: leave with 3 L each. Option: Cime du Diable (2,685 m, +2 h) from the pass. Dinner out 2: L'Estive gîte, open to 1 October; ask where to pitch.

D6THU 17 SEP

Camp d'Argent → Sospel

≈16 km+300 / −1,700 m5–6 hCamping Sainte-Madeleine, ≈€19

Long, steep, hot descent. Start early. Sospel has shops, a bakery and a station on the Nice–Tende line. Dinner out 3: Sospel, by the old bridge. Fill every bottle for tomorrow.

D7FRI 18 SEP

Sospel → Col du Berceau → Menton → train to Nice

≈17 km+1,150 / −1,500 m8–9 htrains to Nice every 20–30 min, 35 min

No water at all in a dry September: 3 L each. The last hour below Col du Berceau (1,090 m) is steep and loose. Then the sea, and the train back to Nice.

§3Rules and water

Park core (Col du Barn to just past Pas du Diable): tent up 19:00–09:00 only, low tent, more than one hour from a road, new spot each night, stove yes, fire no, no dogs.

Merveilles zone: bivouac only on the marked area behind the Refuge des Merveilles. Stay on the trail. Metal pole tips need rubber caps. Wardens fine.

Dry stretches: Merveilles → Camp d'Argent and Sospel → Menton. Nothing mapped, nothing in any report. 3 L each.

§5Practical

September

  • Storms rare, unlike July and August. Valley highs 20–24 °C, 0–5 °C at the high bivouacs, frost possible.
  • First snow on Colomb and Basto is possible from mid-September. If it falls, wait a day.
  • Sunrise ≈07:10, sunset ≈19:45.
  • Live forecast: each day card above shows the current forecast for that day's night spot and high point, from Open-Meteo, refreshed every time the page loads, with warnings derived for thunderstorms, rain, snow and freezing level, ridge wind, frost, heat, fog and UV. The last forecast is kept for offline reading.
  • Official sources to check the day before: Météo-France vigilance, Alpes-Maritimes (colour warnings for storms, rain, heat), Météo-France mountain bulletin, Mercantour (freezing level, wind at 2,000 and 3,000 m, storm timing), park trail notices.
  • Trail notices (park site, 4 Sep): Boréon line reopened; fords at Cougourde and Refuge de Nice. Re-check Info sentiers before leaving.

Transport

  • Nice → Saint-Dalmas: bus 90, weekdays 08:10, 16:40, 18:40 from Les Arboras via Grand Arénas, ≈2 h. Confirm on the Lignes d'Azur app the week before.
  • Menton → Nice: trains every 20–30 min, 35 min.
  • Bail-outs: Madone de Fenestre road, Col de Turini road, Sospel station.

Gear

  • Sleeping system to 0 °C.
  • Poles with rubber tips.
  • 3 L capacity each plus a filter.
  • Four days of food from Nice. Cash for refuges.
  • Offline maps: OsmAnd with the GPX, plus IGN 3741 OT and 3841 OT.
  • Hang food: foxes and boar raid tents by the lakes.
  • Headtorch; you will cook at dusk.
  • Emergency: 112.

§5פרקטי

ספטמבר

  • סופות נדירות, בניגוד ליולי ואוגוסט. בעמקים 20–24 מעלות, בלינות הגבוהות 0–5, ייתכן כפור.
  • שלג ראשון על Colomb ו-Basto אפשרי מאמצע ספטמבר. אם ירד, מחכים יום.
  • זריחה ≈07:10, שקיעה ≈19:45.
  • תחזית חיה: כל כרטיס יום למעלה מציג את התחזית העדכנית לנקודת הלינה ולנקודה הגבוהה של אותו יום, מ-Open-Meteo, מתרעננת בכל טעינה של הדף, עם אזהרות שנגזרות ממנה לסופות רעמים, גשם, שלג וגובה הקיפאון, רוח על הרכס, כפור, חום, ערפל וקרינה. התחזית האחרונה נשמרת לקריאה באופליין.
  • מקורות רשמיים לבדוק יום לפני: Météo-France vigilance, Alpes-Maritimes (אזהרות צבע לסופות, גשם, חום), בולטין ההרים של Météo-France, Mercantour (גובה קיפאון, רוח ב-2,000 ו-3,000 מ', תזמון סופות), הודעות השבילים של הפארק.
  • הודעות שבילים (אתר הפארק, 4.9): הקטע ליד Boréon נפתח מחדש; חציות נחל ליד Cougourde ו-Refuge de Nice. לבדוק שוב ב-Info sentiers לפני היציאה.

תחבורה

  • ניס עד Saint-Dalmas: אוטובוס 90, בימי חול 08:10, 16:40, 18:40 מ-Les Arboras דרך Grand Arénas, כשעתיים. לאשר באפליקציית Lignes d'Azur שבוע לפני.
  • Menton עד ניס: רכבת כל 20–30 דקות, 35 דקות נסיעה.
  • יציאות חירום: הכביש ל-Madone de Fenestre, הכביש ב-Col de Turini, תחנת הרכבת ב-Sospel.
